BMC Geriatrics manuscript and submission figures
Updated 9 November 2026

Manuscript_BMC_Geriatrics_Zotero.docx is the current manuscript, including
the title page, three editable tables, 42 live Zotero citation fields, the
34-reference bibliography, declarations and four figure legends.
Manuscript_BMC_Geriatrics.txt is a plain-text reading copy; it has no live fields.

Upload the four unchanged PNG files in figures/ as separate figures.
The manuscript contains their legends at the end.

nhis-bmc-geriatrics.csl is the document's Zotero citation style. It was installed
and verified in the author's Zotero environment. Install it before refreshing
the document on another computer. references.csl.json contains the 34 reference records embedded in the document.

Scientific wording, results, tables and figure content were preserved in the
journal-format conversion. The structured abstract, headings, spacing, line
and page numbering, declarations and bibliography presentation follow the
BMC Geriatrics research-article requirements. Required declarations use the
author-confirmed information. This repository update does not rerun analyses.
