Historical literature search and first-draft Zotero records

These files document the targeted narrative search and 25-reference evidence set prepared on 6 November 2026. They are retained as research provenance. They do not describe the final reference list or Zotero-field count of the current BMC Geriatrics manuscript.

The current manuscript, its 34-reference bibliography, and its 42 live citation clusters are provided under outputs/bmc_geriatrics/. Use that manuscript for the current wording, author list, declarations and references.

Files
- search_log.txt: search methods, executed queries, selection rationale and limitations of the original narrative search.
- evidence_table.tsv and synthesis.txt: original evidence assessment and background synthesis.
- references.json and metadata_validation.txt: metadata and verification for the original 25-reference evidence set.
- zotero_items.json, zotero_bibliography.txt and zotero_bibliography.html: historical native Zotero exports.
- zotero_manuscript_citations.json and zotero_manuscript_bibliography.txt: citation and bibliography exports for the original manuscript draft.
- Zotero_verification.txt: historical verification of that original draft; its document paths, checksums, field counts and layout observations apply to the document checked at the stated date.

Reference titles, author names, identifiers and embedded Zotero metadata are preserved as recorded. This directory is an audit trail, not a new or updated literature search.
