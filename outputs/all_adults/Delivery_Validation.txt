Revised primary NHIS adult analysis — validation summary
Date: 8 November 2026

Scope: adults aged 18 years and older, followed by a separate central stroke-history analysis. The no-stroke contrast, secondary endpoints and sensitivity comparisons support interpretation. Earlier age-65-plus analyses are historical.

The revised specification was frozen before weighted all-adult outcome estimates were examined. Earlier older-adult results and unweighted adult feasibility counts had been inspected; the specification is not an external preregistration.

Source data: original adult22.csv and adult25.csv plus downloaded ZIP archives were preserved. Byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are checked during packaging against outputs/data_original/original_data_manifest.tsv.

Scientific validation: all nine primary population/stroke/no-stroke levels and year contrasts, including their standard errors, were independently reproduced using weighted arithmetic and explicit PSU-level Taylor covariance without calling the main analysis functions or survey package. Maximum absolute estimate discrepancy: 3.75e-16. Maximum standard-error discrepancy: 3.47e-17. See Independent_Primary_Validation.txt and validation_and_methods.txt.

Reporting validation: manuscript numbers and tables were checked against analysis outputs. The main overall decrease and uncertain principal standardized stroke increase are reported consistently. All graphics are English and use saved estimates; individual artwork dimensions, fonts, resolution and plotting inputs were checked. Four main figures and one supplementary figure are included.

Citation and anonymity validation: 397 checks passed. The identified master retains 19 live Zotero citation clusters, one bibliography field and 25 references. The native Zotero Word Refresh operation completed and was saved. The anonymous manuscript contains static Zotero-generated citations and has no author identifiers in its checked metadata or content. Native PDF exports were used for layout review because LibreOffice was unavailable; final visual checks are recorded in manuscript/Visual_QA.txt.

Limitations: survey collection changes in 2025, outcome missingness, asymmetric respondent-status indicators and sparse younger stroke cells limit interpretation. The estimates are population comparisons of reported symptoms, not causal effects or longitudinal changes. Confidence intervals for supporting analyses are nominal, without multiplicity adjustment.

The archive manifest lists all included files with hashes. Existing study-specific author/declaration checks remain open in outputs/author_checks.txt. No journal submission has been made.
